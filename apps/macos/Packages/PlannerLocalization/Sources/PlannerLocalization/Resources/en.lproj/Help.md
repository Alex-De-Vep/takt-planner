# Takt Help

## Getting started

- Use the sidebar to choose a folder or view, the middle column to pick a task, and the right column to edit it. The calendar opens in an additional panel on the right.
- Click **New Task** in the toolbar or press `⌘N`. Enter a title, then press `Enter` to move to the description. Edits save as you type.
- Click the star to add a task to Today, or the circle next to it to mark it complete.
- Use the toolbar buttons to open the sidebar, calendar, and AI chat. Drag the edge of the right panel to resize it. The speech bubble with a sparkle opens AI chat.
- The welcome screen has four cards with examples for tasks, notes, the calendar, and the AI assistant. To revisit it, choose **Help → Show Welcome**.

## Working with tasks

- Select a task from the list. You can edit its title and description directly in the task pane, including after it is completed.
- Click the circle in the task row or the **Completed** button, or press `⌘Enter` to toggle completion. Repeat the action to reopen the task.
- Completed tasks are grouped under **Completed** at the bottom of the list. Click the header to expand or collapse the group.
- To delete a task with confirmation, open it and click the Trash button in the window toolbar. Pressing `Delete` with focus in the task list deletes the selected task immediately.
- Deleting a task removes its subtasks and links. Its linked events remain in the system calendar.

## Text and subtasks

- Descriptions are plain text. Start a line with `- `, `* `, `• ` or `1. ` and press `Enter` to continue the list. `⇧Enter` inserts a plain line break.
- Use the add-subtask button in the toolbar. Add further items with **Add subtask** or by pressing `Enter` in a filled subtask.
- In a subtask, `⇧Enter` or `⌥Enter` inserts a line break. Pressing `Enter` in an empty item ends the list.
- Use the circle on the left to complete an item and the cross on the right to delete it. The count in the task pane shows how many items are complete.
- To copy the whole task, drag from the title to the last subtask and press `⌘C`. Hold the pointer at the edge of the pane to scroll through a long task. `⌘A` selects text in the active field.
- If an edit could not be saved, use **Retry saving text** next to the field.

## Folders and search

- Each task has one main folder. Use **Inbox**, **This Week**, **Month** or one of your own folders. This Week and Month are permanent folders and keep their tasks across date changes.
- Click the new-folder button in the toolbar or press `⇧⌘N` to create a folder. Right-click a folder to rename or delete it. Deleting a folder moves its tasks to Inbox.
- To move a task, drag its row onto a sidebar folder or click the folder name above the open task’s title and choose another folder.
- **All Tasks** shows the library ordered by creation date. Toolbar search looks in task titles and descriptions within the current view. Clear the search field to see the entire view again.

## Notes

- Open **All Notes** or the **Notes** tab in a folder you created, then click **New Note**. The task/note switch is available in your own folders. The **Create Note** widget also opens a new note.
- Titles and text save after a pause in typing and when you switch notes. Use the tools below the title to format text, add a list, or insert a link. Pasted text adopts the note's font and size.
- Toolbar search filters notes by title and text within the current view. Pin a note to keep it above other notes, or click the pin again to unpin it.
- Drag a note onto a sidebar folder, or click the folder above its title and choose a destination. Notes from a deleted folder remain accessible in **All Notes**.
- When an account is connected, notes sync between devices. Offline edits save on the device and upload when it reconnects. If the same note changes on two devices, a conflicting local edit is kept as a separate copy.
- Audio recordings stay on the device that recorded them. Once inserted, transcript text becomes part of the note and syncs with it. The next section explains recording and transcription.

## Note trash

- The Trash button in a note's toolbar moves it to **Recently Deleted**. Open that view, select the note, and click **Restore** to return it to use.
- You can view a note in the trash. Restore it before editing.
- Thirty days after deletion, Takt clears the note's content and its local audio recordings. Restore a note you need before its retention period ends.
- The deletion state syncs with your account. Delete tasks through their task pane or task list. **Recently Deleted** holds notes.

## Recordings and transcription

- Open a note and click **Record Voice** or **Record Call** in the toolbar. For a call, choose the meeting tab in Chrome and allow audio sharing. The **Microphone** and **Tab** meters in the original note show the audio level from each source.
- Pause, resume, stop, and check the timer in the toolbar even after switching notes. A note can hold multiple recordings. Stop the current recording before starting another.
- The finished recording card shows its duration. Click the button next to the name to edit it, then save. **Show in Finder** takes you to the audio file on your Mac.
- Choose a model in **Settings → Assistant**, then click **Transcribe** on the recording card. Takt stores each recording as one file and sends long audio to the provider in parts after you confirm. If a part fails, you can resume from saved progress. Use **Skip segment** when available to continue without that segment.
- When the transcript is ready, a preview opens. Click **Insert into note** to append the text and jump to it in the original note. If you closed the preview, click **Open transcript** on the recording card.

## Today

- Click the star in a task row or the **Today** button in the task pane, or press `⇧⌘S`. Repeat the action to remove the star.
- Drag a task onto **Today** to star it. The task stays in its main folder.
- A star stays on across midnight until you remove it. Deadlines are set separately.
- A new task created in Today is placed in Inbox with its star already on.

## macOS widgets

- In the macOS widget gallery, choose **Takt → Today** and add the small or medium widget. It shows active starred tasks. Clicking the widget opens Takt.
- Takt updates the widget's task list when the library changes. macOS controls when the widget refreshes on screen. If the widget asks you to load your tasks, open Takt.
- Add the small **Create Note** and **Create Event** widgets from Takt in the widget gallery. The mint widget opens a new note for editing. If no folder is selected, the note appears in **All Notes**. The blue widget opens a new event for today. Takt may first ask you to connect your calendars.

## Task deadlines

- Open a task and click the clock button in the toolbar. Pick a date, or use **Today** or **Tomorrow**, then click **Save**.
- Turn on **Include time** to add an hour and minute. **Remove deadline** clears the date and cancels its reminder.
- A deadline is when the task is due. To reserve a block of time in your schedule, create a linked calendar event.

## Notifications

- In **Settings → Notifications**, you can turn on deadline notifications and a separate reminder to review your tasks. The review reminder is off by default. Choose a time and optionally restrict it to weekdays.
- A timed deadline triggers a notification at the selected time. Date-only deadlines use the time in Settings, which defaults to 9:00 AM.
- Changing a deadline reschedules the notification. Completing or deleting the task, or clearing the deadline, cancels a pending reminder. Reopening the task schedules the reminder again if its deadline is still ahead.
- Allow notifications in macOS. **Test notification** sends a test after five seconds. Banners and sounds depend on system settings and Focus mode.
- Clicking a deadline notification opens its task. macOS delivers scheduled notifications even when Takt is closed.

## Calendar and agenda

- Open the right panel with the calendar button in the toolbar and click **Connect calendar**. Viewing and editing events requires full access to your system calendars.
- Pick a date in the month grid. Use the arrows to change the month or agenda period and **Today** to return to the current date. A dot below a date means it has events.
- Choose **Day**, **3 days** or **Week**. In the three-day and week views, you can switch between a vertical list and horizontal columns.
- In **Settings → Calendar**, choose which calendars to show and where to create new events. You can change calendar access in macOS System Settings.

## Calendar events

- Pick a date and click **Event**. Enter a title, start and end, and choose a calendar. You can turn on **All day** and add notes.
- Add one or more alerts relative to the event’s start or at a specific date and time. The system calendar delivers these alerts. An alert set for a specific date keeps its time when the event moves.
- Click an event in the agenda to open it. You can edit or delete one-off events in writable calendars. Recurring events and events in read-only calendars can be viewed.
- To move a one-off event to a different day, choose **3 days** or **Week** and drag the event to the desired day. Click **Undo move** if needed.

## Planning in the calendar

- Open a task, choose a date in the calendar and click **For task**. Set a time and save the event. You can reserve more than one time block for a task.
- Linked events appear under **Scheduled** in the task pane. Click an event there to open it, or click the linked-task icon in the agenda to return to the task.
- Tasks and events are independent. Deleting an event keeps its task, and deleting a task keeps its events in the system calendar.

## Account and sync

### Signing in and copying tasks

- Choose **Continue on This Mac** on the welcome screen to use a local library. You can connect an account later in **Settings → Account**.
- To create an account, enter your email and password and verify your email with the eight-digit code. Sign in to the same account on another Mac. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot Password?** and use the email code to set a new one.
- If Takt asks whether to copy your current tasks when you sign in, choose **Copy Tasks and Sign In** or **Sign In Without Copying**. Your existing local tasks and the account library can be kept separately.

### Working offline and signing out

- Edits save on your Mac and are sent when you connect. The bottom of the sidebar shows when sync is running or up to date.
- Click **Sync failed** to see the reason and pending changes. Click **Sync Now** to retry, or sign in again if prompted. You can also check the status in **Settings → Account**.
- After you sign out, the open library remains on your Mac. Sync resumes when you sign in to the same account again. System calendars and links to calendar events are handled separately from task sync.

## AI chat

### Start a conversation

- Open the right panel with the speech bubble and sparkle button. For your first connection, click **Connect**, or open **Settings → Assistant → Connect an AI provider**.
- After connecting, add models to **Selected models**. Choose a model at the bottom of the chat and send a message with the arrow button. While a reply is being generated, use the same button to **Stop** generation.
- Click **New conversation** for a separate topic. **History** opens previous conversations. Clearing history removes conversations and their proposals. Tasks, events, and edits you have already saved remain in the library.
- Try “Break holiday preparation into tasks”, “Shorten the open note”, or “Draft a team meeting for tomorrow at 10:00”.

### Folder, task, and note context

- The **Message context** menu next to the model selector contains **Use folder**, **Use task**, and **Use note**. Check the attached context before sending. Open the task or note you want to use first.
- Context helps the assistant choose a destination folder and work with the open record's text. After switching tasks or notes, check the context of your next message.
- Your message, the current conversation's history, and attached context are sent to the selected AI provider to produce a response. The connection determines which service handles the request.

### Proposals and edits

- The assistant can draft tasks with subtasks, calendar events, and text edits. Available actions depend on the selected model and its tool support. Allow Takt calendar access to work with events.
- Review the proposal card. Choose **Edit** to adjust its title, description, subtasks, folder, or event details, then save the proposal and add it using the card's button. Find proposals under **Chat tasks** or **Chat cards**.
- Review a task edit and click **Apply**. If the original task has changed, choose **Refresh from current text**.
- A note proposal offers **Replace note text** and **Append to Note**. Choose an action after reviewing the text. **Cancel** dismisses a pending edit.
- The small button at the bottom right of a message copies its full text. Drag to select and copy a fragment.

## Choosing AI models

- In **Settings → Assistant**, connect services and manage **Selected models**. Add models you can access, then choose one in the chat menu. Settings and the chat panel share the selected chat model.
- **Customize model list…** opens model settings from the chat. If you remove the current model, choose another before sending the next message. Connect a service first, then select its models.
- Choose the **Transcription model** separately. Supported models can come from OpenRouter, LiteLLM, GigaChat, OpenAI API, Gemini, Mistral, and Grok. Availability depends on the connected account and Takt's catalog.
- When **Reasoning effort** is available, choose it next to the chat model. Options depend on the model. Usage and limits are shown for connections that provide this information.

## Connecting AI providers

### General steps

- Open **Settings → Assistant → Connect an AI provider** and choose a service. For a key-based connection, fill in the fields and click **Verify and Connect**. Once connected, add a model to **Selected models** and send a short chat message.
- **Get a key** opens the service's account portal. Where **Model IDs** is shown, enter one to eight model IDs available to your account, separated by commas. Checking these models sends short requests that may incur provider charges.
- Click a connected service to manage it. Use **Update connection** to replace a key. Provider keys are stored in macOS Keychain.

### ChatGPT

- Choose **ChatGPT**, click **Continue with ChatGPT**, finish signing in through your browser, and return to Takt. Your account and available models appear after authorization.
- Use the account menu to **Add a ChatGPT account**, select a saved account, or **Sign out of ChatGPT**. **Limits** opens plan usage in ChatGPT. Your account determines available models and quotas. [ChatGPT account](https://chatgpt.com/).

### OpenRouter

- Choose **OpenRouter** and click **Sign in with OpenRouter**. Sign in through your browser, authorize the connection, and return to Takt. Takt obtains a key through browser authorization and loads the model catalog. [OpenRouter account](https://openrouter.ai/), [official connection guide](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/overview/auth/oauth).
- Select models in Takt settings. Access to paid models depends on the balance and limits of the issued key. Manage them in OpenRouter. [OpenRouter keys](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys).

### LiteLLM

- Ask your LiteLLM administrator for the gateway address and a virtual key with access to the models you need. If you manage the gateway, create a key under **Virtual Keys → Create New Key**. [Keys and model access](https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/proxy/access_management/llm).
- In Takt, choose **LiteLLM**, enter the **LiteLLM address** and **LiteLLM virtual key**, then click **Verify and Connect**. Use HTTPS for a server, or HTTP for a service on this Mac. Model names come from your gateway's configuration.

### GitHub Copilot

- Prepare a GitHub account with Copilot access and install Node.js 22.12 or newer. Choose **GitHub Copilot** in Takt, click **Copy Launch Command**, and run it in Terminal on this Mac. [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/), [GitHub Copilot authentication](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/copilot-cli/set-up-copilot-cli/authenticate-copilot-cli).
- Follow the local service's prompts and sign in to GitHub through your browser. Paste the resulting **Local Connection Code** into Takt, then click **Verify and Connect**. Keep the Terminal service running while you use the connection.

### MiniMax M Plan

- In your MiniMax account, open **Billing → M Plan** and obtain the plan's **Subscription Key**. Access depends on assigned plan resources or Credits. [M Plan](https://www.minimax.io/m-plan), [getting a Subscription Key](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/quickstart-preparation).
- In Takt, choose **MiniMax M Plan**, enter the **M Plan Subscription Key** and model IDs included in your plan, then click **Verify and Connect**.

### MiniMax API

- Sign in to MiniMax API Platform, open **API Keys**, and select **Create new secret key**. Add API billing resources if needed. [MiniMax API keys](https://platform.minimax.io/console/keys), [MiniMax setup guide](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/quickstart-preparation).
- In Takt, choose **MiniMax API**, enter the **API Key** and **Model IDs**, then click **Verify and Connect**. Use model IDs available through this key.

### Claude

- Sign in to Claude Console, open **API keys**, and create a key for your workspace. Copy the key when it is created and check API access for that workspace. [Claude keys](https://platform.claude.com/settings/keys), [Anthropic's setup guide](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/get-api-key).
- In Takt, choose **Claude**, enter the **API Key**, and click **Verify and Connect**. Add an available Claude model to Selected models.

### Gemini

- Open Google AI Studio with your Google account, go to **API keys**, and create or select a key for your project. Check that the project has Gemini API access. [Google AI Studio keys](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey), [Google's setup guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/api-key).
- In Takt, choose **Gemini**, enter the **API Key**, and click **Verify and Connect**. Choose chat and transcription models in Assistant settings after connecting.

### DeepSeek

- Sign in to DeepSeek Platform, open **API keys**, and create a key. Check your API access and balance in the account portal. [DeepSeek keys](https://platform.deepseek.com/api_keys), [DeepSeek's setup guide](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/).
- In Takt, choose **DeepSeek**, enter the **API Key**, click **Verify and Connect**, and select a model from the loaded catalog.

### Mistral

- Sign in to the Mistral console, open **API keys**, and create a workspace key. Save its value when it is created. [Mistral keys](https://console.mistral.ai/api-keys), [Mistral's setup guide](https://docs.mistral.ai/getting-started/quickstarts/studio/activate-and-generate-api-key).
- In Takt, choose **Mistral**, enter the **API Key**, click **Verify and Connect**, and add the models you need. Choose a supported transcription model separately in settings.

### GLM · Z.ai

- Sign in to Z.ai, open **API Keys**, and create a **Standard API Key**. Check model IDs against the standard API's catalog. [Z.ai keys](https://z.ai/manage-apikey/apikey-list), [Z.ai's setup guide](https://docs.z.ai/guides/overview/quick-start).
- In Takt, choose **GLM · Z.ai**, enter the **API Key** and **Model IDs**, then click **Verify and Connect**. This form uses the standard Z.ai API at `https://api.z.ai/api/paas/v4`.

### Grok

- Sign in to the xAI console, select a team, and create a key under **API Keys**. Check the team's model access and API billing. [xAI console](https://console.x.ai/), [xAI's setup guide](https://docs.x.ai/developers/quickstart).
- In Takt, choose **Grok**, enter the **API Key**, click **Verify and Connect**, and add an available model to Selected models.

### QwenCloud

- In the QwenCloud console, select a region, open **API Keys**, and create a pay-as-you-go key. Save the key and model IDs available in that region. [QwenCloud console](https://console.qwencloud.com/), [getting an API key](https://docs.qwencloud.com/api-reference/preparation/api-key).
- In Takt, choose **QwenCloud** and the matching **API Region**, enter the **API Key** and **Model IDs**, then click **Verify and Connect**. Takt's region must match the key's region. [QwenCloud regions](https://docs.qwencloud.com/developer-guides/getting-started/regions).

### Kimi

- Sign in to Kimi API Platform and create a key under **API Keys**. Use the account portal and region associated with your API access. [Getting a Kimi key](https://platform.kimi.ai/docs/get-your-api-key).
- In Takt, choose **Kimi**, select the matching **API Region**, enter the **API Key**, and click **Verify and Connect**. Add a model from the returned catalog to Selected models.

### YandexGPT Pro

- In Yandex Cloud, select a cloud and folder. Create a service account with access to models in that folder and an API key for text generation. Text generation uses the `ai.languageModels.user` role and the `yc.ai.languageModels.execute` key scope. Copy the secret key and folder ID. [Yandex Cloud console](https://console.yandex.cloud/), [creating an API key](https://yandex.cloud/ru/docs/iam/operations/authentication/manage-api-keys), [configuring model access](https://yandex.cloud/ru/docs/tutorials/security/traces-for-litellm-proxy).
- In Takt, choose **YandexGPT Pro**, enter the **API Key** and **Yandex Cloud folder ID**, then click **Verify and Connect**. Takt sends a short request to check YandexGPT Pro in the chosen folder.

### GigaChat

- Sign in to Sber's developer Studio and open a **GigaChat API** project. Under **API settings**, click **Get key** and save the **Authorization Key** value. [Studio account](https://developers.sber.ru/studio/), [getting a GigaChat key](https://developers.sber.ru/docs/ru/gigachat/quickstart/ind-using-api).
- In Takt, choose **GigaChat** and paste the full Authorization Key into **GigaChat Authorization Key**. Takt obtains and refreshes temporary access tokens using this key.
- Under **GigaChat API Edition**, choose your project's access type — **Personal** (`GIGACHAT_API_PERS`), **Business (B2B)** (`GIGACHAT_API_B2B`), or **Corporate (CORP)** (`GIGACHAT_API_CORP`). Click **Verify and Connect**, then add a model from the catalog. The access type must match the project and its current terms. [Individual access](https://developers.sber.ru/docs/ru/gigachat/individuals-quickstart), [business access](https://developers.sber.ru/docs/ru/gigachat/legal-quickstart).

### OpenAI API

- Sign in to OpenAI Platform, select a project, and create a key on the **API keys** page. Check the project's access to the models and API resources you need. [OpenAI keys](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys), [OpenAI's setup guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/quickstart).
- In Takt, choose **OpenAI API**, enter the **API Key**, and click **Verify and Connect**. Choose a chat or transcription model in Assistant settings after connecting.

### If the connection fails

- For an authentication error, check the key value, expiration, account, API Region, and access type. For GigaChat, check the Authorization Key and API version. For YandexGPT, check the key and folder. For QwenCloud, match the key and endpoint regions.
- For a model error, check its exact ID and access through the selected key. Compare manually entered IDs with the provider's portal. In LiteLLM, use the names configured in your gateway.
- If a quota or balance is exhausted, open the service's usage and billing pages. If a provider asks you to retry later, wait for its stated interval and try the connection again.
- If browser sign-in does not finish, return to Takt, cancel the attempt, and start sign-in again. For GitHub Copilot, check that the local service in Terminal is still running.

## Backup and support

- In **Settings → Backup**, save a local copy of the library to a file. Restoring opens a separate library on this Mac. The backup file is unencrypted.
- Click **Settings → Contact us** next to Help and sign in if prompted. Your report includes your Takt and macOS versions, your device time with its time zone, and your reply email.
- After sending, use **Copy code** to keep the receipt code. If sending is interrupted, Takt keeps your text so you can retry.

## Settings and shortcuts

- Open Settings from **Takt → Settings…** or with `⌘,`. Change the interface language under **Account**. Your task and folder names stay as entered.
- The **Help** button at the bottom of the Settings sidebar opens this guide.
- Takt follows the macOS light or dark appearance. Use System Settings to manage calendar and notification permissions.
- `⌘N` creates a task. `⇧⌘N` creates a folder. `⇧⌘S` toggles the star. `⌘Enter` toggles completion of the selected task.
- `⌘C`, `⌘V`, `⌘Z` and `⇧⌘Z` copy, paste, undo and redo. `Esc` dismisses a dialog or clears the active search field.
