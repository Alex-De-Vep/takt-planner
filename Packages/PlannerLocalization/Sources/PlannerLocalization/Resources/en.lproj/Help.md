# Planner Help

## Getting started

- Use the sidebar to choose a folder or view, the middle column to pick a task, and the right column to edit it. The calendar opens in an additional panel on the right.
- Click **New Task** in the toolbar or press `⌘N`. Enter a title, then press `Enter` to move to the description. Edits save as you type.
- Click the star to add a task to Today, or the circle next to it to mark it complete.
- Use the toolbar buttons to show or hide the folder and calendar panels. Drag the edge of the calendar panel to resize it.
- To revisit the first-run screen, choose **Help → Show Welcome**.

## Working with tasks

- Select a task from the list. You can edit its title and description directly in the task pane, including after it is completed.
- Click the circle in the task row or the **Completed** button, or press `⌘Enter` to toggle completion. Repeat the action to reopen the task.
- Completed tasks are grouped under **Completed** at the bottom of the list. Click the header to expand or collapse the group.
- Choose **Delete Task…** from the row’s context menu or the **…** menu above the task to confirm deletion. Pressing `Delete` with focus in the task list deletes the selected task immediately.
- Deleting a task removes its subtasks and links. Its linked events remain in the system calendar.

## Text and subtasks

- Descriptions are plain text. Start a line with `- `, `* `, `• ` or `1. ` and press `Enter` to continue the list. `⇧Enter` inserts a plain line break.
- Use the add-subtask button in the toolbar. Add further items with **Add subtask** or by pressing `Enter` in a filled subtask.
- In a subtask, `⇧Enter` or `⌥Enter` inserts a line break. Pressing `Enter` in an empty item ends the list.
- Use the circle on the left to complete an item and the cross on the right to delete it. The count in the task pane shows how many items are complete.
- To copy the whole task, drag from the title to the last subtask and press `⌘C`. Hold the pointer at the edge of the pane to scroll through a long task. `⌘A` selects text in the active field.
- If an edit could not be saved, use **Retry saving text** next to the field.

## Folders and search

- Each task has one main folder. Use **Inbox**, **This Week**, **Month** or one of your own folders. This Week and Month are permanent folders and keep their tasks across date changes.
- Click the new-folder button in the toolbar or press `⇧⌘N` to create a folder. Right-click a folder to rename or delete it. Deleting a folder moves its tasks to Inbox.
- To move a task, drag its row onto a sidebar folder, or choose **Move to Folder** from the row’s context menu or the **…** menu above the task.
- **All Tasks** shows the library ordered by creation date. Toolbar search looks in task titles and descriptions within the current view. Clear the search field to see the entire view again.

## Today

- Click the star in a task row or the **Today** button in the task pane, or press `⇧⌘S`. Repeat the action to remove the star.
- Drag a task onto **Today** to star it. The task stays in its main folder.
- A star stays on across midnight until you remove it. Deadlines are set separately.
- A new task created in Today is placed in Inbox with its star already on.

## Task deadlines

- Open a task and click the clock button in the toolbar. Pick a date, or use **Today** or **Tomorrow**, then click **Save**.
- Turn on **Include time** to add an hour and minute. **Remove deadline** clears the date and cancels its reminder.
- A deadline is when the task is due. To reserve a block of time in your schedule, create a linked calendar event.

## Notifications

- In **Settings → Notifications**, you can turn on deadline notifications and a separate reminder to review your tasks. The review reminder is off by default. Choose a time and optionally restrict it to weekdays.
- A timed deadline triggers a notification at the selected time. Date-only deadlines use the time in Settings, which defaults to 9:00 AM.
- Changing a deadline reschedules the notification. Completing or deleting the task, or clearing the deadline, cancels a pending reminder. Reopening the task schedules the reminder again if its deadline is still ahead.
- Allow notifications in macOS. **Test notification** sends a test after five seconds. Banners and sounds depend on system settings and Focus mode.
- Clicking a deadline notification opens its task. macOS delivers scheduled notifications even when Planner is closed.

## Calendar and agenda

- Open the right panel with the calendar button in the toolbar and click **Connect calendar**. Viewing and editing events requires full access to your system calendars.
- Pick a date in the month grid. Use the arrows to change the month or agenda period and **Today** to return to the current date. A dot below a date means it has events.
- Choose **Day**, **3 days** or **Week**. In the three-day and week views, you can switch between a vertical list and horizontal columns.
- In **Settings → Calendar**, choose which calendars to show and where to create new events. You can change calendar access in macOS System Settings.

## Calendar events

- Pick a date and click **Event**. Enter a title, start and end, and choose a calendar. You can turn on **All day** and add notes.
- Add one or more alerts relative to the event’s start or at a specific date and time. The system calendar delivers these alerts. An alert set for a specific date keeps its time when the event moves.
- Click an event in the agenda to open it. You can edit or delete one-off events in writable calendars. Recurring events and events in read-only calendars can be viewed.
- To move a one-off event to a different day, choose **3 days** or **Week** and drag the event to the desired day. Click **Undo move** if needed.

## Planning in the calendar

- Open a task, choose a date in the calendar and click **For task**. Set a time and save the event. You can reserve more than one time block for a task.
- Linked events appear under **Scheduled** in the task pane. Click an event there to open it, or click the linked-task icon in the agenda to return to the task.
- Tasks and events are independent. Deleting an event keeps its task, and deleting a task keeps its events in the system calendar.

## Account and sync

### Signing in and copying tasks

- Choose **Continue on This Mac** on the welcome screen to use a local library. You can connect an account later in **Settings → Account**.
- To create an account, enter your email and password and verify your email with the eight-digit code. Sign in to the same account on another Mac. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot Password?** and use the email code to set a new one.
- If Planner asks whether to copy your current tasks when you sign in, choose **Copy Tasks and Sign In** or **Sign In Without Copying**. Your existing local tasks and the account library can be kept separately.

### Working offline and signing out

- Changes are saved on your Mac and sent when you connect. Check the sync status and pending changes under **Account**. Click **Sync Now** to sync manually.
- After you sign out, the open library remains on your Mac. Sync resumes when you sign in to the same account again. System calendars and links to calendar events are handled separately from task sync.

## Settings and shortcuts

- Open Settings from the bottom of the folder sidebar or with `⌘,`. Change the interface language under **Account**. Your task and folder names stay as entered.
- The **Help** button at the bottom of the Settings sidebar opens this guide.
- Planner follows the macOS light or dark appearance. Use System Settings to manage calendar and notification permissions.
- `⌘N` creates a task. `⇧⌘N` creates a folder. `⇧⌘S` toggles the star. `⌘Enter` toggles completion of the selected task.
- `⌘C`, `⌘V`, `⌘Z` and `⇧⌘Z` copy, paste, undo and redo. `Esc` dismisses a dialog or clears the active search field.
